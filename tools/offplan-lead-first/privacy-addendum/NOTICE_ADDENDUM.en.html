Optional measurement and your choices

You can choose analytics and advertising measurement separately. Analytics measures visits and enquiry interactions. Advertising measurement links ad clicks with accepted enquiries. Personalized advertising is off. These choices are separate from permission to contact you about an enquiry and from optional property news and offers. You can submit an enquiry without optional measurement.

When you allow measurement, Google may receive online identifiers and measurement information, including your IP address, for the selected purpose. Read how Google uses information from businesses that use its services.

Your measurement preferences remain valid for up to 180 days. Open Measurement preferences on an enquiry page to change your choices or reject optional measurement. Withdrawal stops further optional measurement; it does not cancel your enquiry.

If you do not submit an enquiry, records of a measurement choice are retained for 12 months after that choice. Where permission evidence is linked to an enquiry, it follows our enquiry-retention policy. Closed or unsuccessful enquiries and corresponding Marketing email copies are reviewed monthly and deleted once 12 months have passed since closure. Preference expiry is separate from record deletion.

For privacy or deletion requests, contact marketing@blackswanrealestate.ae.
