## Authority and transports

The existing WordPress receiver URL and lead HMAC authenticate the new control transport only. The control envelope has exactly `control_contract`, `control_version`, `timestamp`, `challenge`, `operation`, `actor`, `body` and `signature`. Its contract is `bsre-receiver-control-v1`; the version is integer 1. `body` and the entire envelope must be canonical JSON. The HMAC message is the contract, timestamp, challenge, operation, actor and body joined with newlines. The only control verbs are `inspect`, `retire`, `readback`, `prepare_cleanup` and `forget`. Original wire version 1 always takes the original lead parser.

The receiver separately requires protected `BSRE_ACTUAL_RETIREMENT_ENABLED=true`, `BSRE_RECEIVER_AUTHORITY_URL`, `BSRE_RECEIVER_AUTHORITY_SECRET`, `BSRE_RECEIVER_NAMESPACE`, `BSRE_RECEIVER_SOURCE_SHA256` and `BSRE_RECEIVER_EVIDENCE_KEY_ID`. No values are supplied. The authority secret must differ from the WordPress-known lead secret. Its fresh request/response MAC domains are `bsre-receiver-authority-request-v1` and `bsre-receiver-authority-response-v1`. Requests bind namespace, source, operation, nonce, issued time and exact arguments; responses additionally bind the request digest and fresh expiry. The receiver never accepts a caller-supplied URL, key, namespace or source version.

The receiver-only RPC verbs are `runtime.current`, `runtime.advance_retirement`, `runtime.advance_prune`, `runtime.cleanup_receipt`, `mailbox.current_including_trash`, `owner_intent.current` and `attest_control_result`. The service must implement their exact consumed closed schemas. In particular, it changes only its independent epoch/floor; the receiver validates the complete next gate and writes/readbacks its own Properties under ScriptLock. The durable order is remote CAS, local floor, sender fence, physical RETIRED row, physical metadata. Any uncertain acknowledgement or partial transition holds; there is no automatic recovery/reset endpoint.

PHP additionally requires an enrolled independent Ed25519 public key from `BSRE_RECEIVER_EVIDENCE_PUBLIC_KEYS`. The receiver service returns canonical `{key_id,statement,signature}`. Its signed statement is exactly `{contract,service,operation,scope,source,actor,challenge,request_hash,issued_at,expires_at,claims}`. The contract is `bsre-actual-receiver-evidence-v1`, service `receiver-retirement`, scope is the original Workflow digest of the operation scope, and source is `{receiver,namespace,admission,generation}`. The signed bytes are UTF8 hexadecimal Workflow digest of the statement. Signature encoding is canonical base64. Claims are exactly `{result,intent_digest,current_history,receiver_only}`; the last two must be actual booleans true. Maximum issued age is 30 seconds and validity 60 seconds. Missing key enrolment is checked before mutable transport. Shared lead HMAC is never this authority.

The independent signer must authenticate receiver-only provenance, independently current history, exact source and admission version, actor/action/durable intent, identity/status/attempt state, complete Marketing mailbox including Trash, and snapshot/restore floor. `attest_control_result` is not permission to blindly sign an observation array. The source observation must be reconciled with the independent service's current authoritative state. No signer/server implementation or complete provider dataset has been invented in this candidate.

All proof verification clocks come from the trusted local process after remote I/O. Entry timestamps are checked at entry; intermediate phases use fresh local times. Original plan approval, create deadline and recovery deadline are never renewed by elapsed I/O, retries or proof issuance.

Stable data/anchor identity uses unchanged RuntimePolicy scope: database, prefix and origin. It excludes actor and code hash. The separately configured receiver namespace must remain stable through routine releases. Fresh source/mail/history/snapshot custody is required on every destructive use, even when the ticket is historical. This does not itself provide an actual purge-plan producer.
