## 1. Authority, supersession and commercial objective

The authority order is: the owner's latest explicit instructions; previously applicable owner instructions not superseded; verified live operational facts; the uploaded dossier; and then clearly labeled design proposals. A source cannot turn a historical draft into a current approval.

**Superseded:** 200 positive and 200 negative keywords per campaign; million-placement generation targets as the delivery requirement; 30 separate spending campaigns per landing page; generic investor-only positioning; and any blanket exclusion of residential rental or commercial demand.

**Preserved:** 300 original real-estate page slots plus the 60-slot rental/leasing/seller/landlord expansion; all-UAE coverage; decision-stage and upstream intent; developer/project/portal/event research; separate campaign destinations; first-party measurement; no-CRM implementation boundary; immutable lead IDs; email plus Google Sheet routing; collected-commission economics; independent QA; preservation of useful existing assets; and explicit release approval.

The source dossier's 30 intent dimensions remain a research checklist. Thus 360 × 30 = 10,800 evaluation cells, not 10,800 campaigns, pages or additional keyword quotas. A cell can be relevant, consolidated, held or not applicable. Do not force every dimension onto every page.

**Commercial objective:** maximize collected company contribution from legitimate brokerage transactions, while creating reusable inventory intelligence, decision tools, listing supply and consented repeat/referral relationships. Do not optimize for the largest keyword count, the cheapest click, the most forms or the greatest number of generated pages.

### Keyword generation and admission algorithm

```text
INPUTS:
  approved service scope; seven-emirate coverage; current inventory/mandates;
  page registry; source terms; historic Ads/search data if accessible;
  actual customer questions; approved languages; protected-intent tests.

FOR each page family:
  identify its real brokerage decision and the appropriate audience role
  select signals that genuinely apply
  collect seed language from authorized sources and customer/search evidence
  create only meaningful variants of transaction, property, place, constraint,
      timing, budget structure, comparison and decision stage
  attach source type: OBSERVED_QUERY / VERIFIED_ENTITY / CUSTOMER_LANGUAGE /
      RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS
  normalize, deduplicate and identify semantic overlap
  assign one primary campaign owner, ad group, dedicated page and intent tier
  reject incompatible or awkward combinations, invented entities and wrong services
  mark unknown volume/CPC as UNKNOWN, never 0 or an invented estimate
  test against every exclusion that would apply in that campaign
  require inventory/service, language, landing-page and claim review
  retain approved research records up to the family allocation
  archive all rejections and replacements with reasons

RECONCILE:
  completed master = 14,000 distinct nonblank phrases
  generation, review, deployment and active status are separate counts
```

Do not append every emirate to every project name. A Dubai project does not become an Abu Dhabi project because a keyword generator changes the suffix. Audience-origin modifiers can differ from property destination, but that relationship must be explicit.

Begin deployment with exact/phrase hypotheses and controlled expansion. Positive exact match is not a promise of literal-only query matching. Broad matching, AI Max matching and other expansion are distinct experiments and require clean qualification feedback, budget controls and approval. Do not let automatic recommendations silently change the release.

Use primarily English and Arabic where there is a complete ad–page–sales experience. Other languages require real support and review. They fit inside the 14,000 total unless the owner changes scope; they are not an automatic extra 14,000 per language.

### Scope and approval ladder

1. Account-wide: only unmistakably incompatible intent across every currently served segment. Use a small reviewed core, not all 14,000.
2. Shared lists: reusable, purpose-defined exclusions attached only to eligible campaigns.
3. Campaign exclusions: wrong transaction, product or location *for this campaign*, preserving appropriate demand elsewhere.
4. Ad-group routing: tightly scoped only where it resolves a demonstrable overlap without blocking useful long-tail searches.
5. High-risk review: geography, price, competitor, regulator, education, job-related single words, complaints and ownership-process terms.
6. Protected/rejected: retain in a separate audit register; do not deploy or count as accepted negative phrases.

Google documents 5,000 terms per shared negative list, 20 shared lists per account and 10,000 campaign negatives; inventory-specific limits also differ. The central 14,000-row research database is not one platform list and does not authorize attaching all rows to each campaign. Recheck actual account/API limits before deployment. Source: G2.

A proposed shared-list layout is jobs/applications, career/salary research, courses/academic, software/vendor, media/unrelated product, and tightly reviewed navigation/support. Split only when useful and within limits. Keep campaign routing rules separate. Do not assume these controls behave identically in Search, Display, Video and Performance Max.

Google's current negative guidance distinguishes broad, phrase and exact matching; negatives do not expand to all close variants. It also says casing and misspellings are handled automatically. Do not count those as valuable additional rows. Source: G3.

### Conflict and deployment algorithm

```text
FOR each candidate negative:
  read the complete query and intended scope
  identify the rejected commercial need, not just an isolated token
  ask whether any BlackSwan brokerage segment can legitimately serve it
  check for buyer, seller, landlord, tenant, occupier or authorized partner intent
  check whether poor outcome might be missing tracking or inadequate follow-up
  choose KEEP / INVESTIGATE / PROPOSE_NARROW_NEGATIVE
  when proposing:
    choose the smallest effective phrase and scope
    test all protected positive phrases and representative queries in that scope
    test long-tail and cross-segment counterexamples
    if any unresolved valuable conflict: HOLD
    otherwise submit for independent approval

FOR each approved deployment:
  snapshot existing negatives and list associations
  compile a minimal-diff change manifest
  validate limits, applicability and expected affected campaigns
  apply only within explicit permission
  read back platform objects; compare with manifest
  monitor lost qualified coverage, not just fewer clicks
  retain one-step rollback and reviewer history
```

Use an inverted token index and scope index to avoid naively comparing every negative against every positive repeatedly. A local tokenizer simulator is a conservative regression tool, not Google's actual serving engine. Validate uncertain cases with real account diagnostics and disclosed queries.

## 7. Lead-quality controls beyond negatives

Keywords alone cannot establish whether a visitor is serious. Add a low-friction intent selector before the property enquiry flow: Buy / Invest / Rent / Sell / Let a property / Commercial premises / Other enquiry.

Provide a separate, clearly labeled employment route. A job applicant must not enter a property-buying conversion merely because they complete a general contact form. Connect that route only to a real approved careers/contact destination; do not invent a live HR URL or email.

Use server validation, honeypots, rate limits, duplicate detection and risk-based bot challenges. An unusually fast submission or repeated IP is a signal, not automatic proof of a fake lead; shared networks and legitimate repeat enquiries need review. OTP is a testable friction choice, not a universal requirement. Offer a usable alternative where channel verification fails.

Suggested lead states:

```text

GENUINE_NURTURE
GENUINE_NO_CURRENT_INVENTORY
OWNER_MANDATE_POTENTIAL
AUTHORIZED_PARTNER
EMPLOYMENT_ENQUIRY
```

Keep fit, contactability, intent and readiness separate. Never convert a single unanswered call into a confirmed bad lead. Sales must provide timestamped attempt evidence, channel outcome, discovery notes, promised callback and next action before rejecting reachable demand.

For lead-quality reporting, disclose the denominator. 'Employment rate' can mean employment inquiries divided by all durably accepted inquiries; it is not a click fraud rate or an estimate of all undisclosed search queries.

**Unlisted is not private.** Ads, saved links and shared links can open the page. A visitor does not have to type the URL manually. Noindex controls search inclusion, not access permissions. Password protection would serve a different purpose and interfere with this public ad-click journey. Source: G1.

### Visibility implementation

- Exclude campaign routes from main navigation, public page directories, site search, XML/HTML sitemaps, RSS and automated related-content indexes.
- Add `<meta name="robots" content="noindex,follow">` in the server-rendered HTML, or use an appropriate `X-Robots-Tag` header. Test query-parameter and language variants.
- Allow crawling necessary to read noindex and review the destination. Do not block `/lp/` in robots.txt or deny AdsBot at the CDN/WAF.
- Do not require a GCLID, ad referrer, secret cookie, login or special user agent to see the truthful page.
- Do not serve different misleading offers to reviewers and visitors.
- Use a neutral self-canonical where the stack requires one; do not canonicalize all pages to the homepage or treat canonical tags as a privacy mechanism.
- Keep staging authenticated and distinct from production; production ad pages remain openly accessible but unlisted.

Google must be able to crawl a page to see its noindex instruction. Source: G1. Ads destinations also need to meet Google's functionality, usefulness and accessibility requirements. Source: G5.

### Main-website benefit without misleading SEO claims

These pages are already on the main domain. Reuse the same approved design system, consent layer, lawful analytics configuration and stable inventory components. A visitor can click the logo, relevant property collections, company information and useful public guides. Measure these as on-site continuation; do not fabricate a second visitor or a homepage view.

Noindex pages are not the organic-search content strategy, and paying for ads does not buy organic ranking. Publish genuinely useful, separately reviewed public community guides, cost tools and property explanations when organic/AI-search visibility is desired. Source: G6. Do not copy hundreds of nearly identical ad pages into the public index.

Keep paid attribution when people move into the main site. Do not add fresh UTMs to internal links or overwrite the original source with an internal promotion tag. Use an internal event such as `lp_to_main_site` with page IDs rather than a new acquisition campaign.

### Enforce one campaign, one designated entry page

## 16. Commercial economics and budget allocation

```text
Collected net brokerage revenue
  = commission actually collected - VAT - refunds - true pass-through amounts

Contribution before acquisition
  = collected net brokerage revenue
    - agent payout - referral payout - direct transaction/fulfilment costs

Allowable media CAC
  = contribution before acquisition per customer/transaction
    - non-media acquisition cost
    - required retained contribution

Allowable CPQL
  = allowable media CAC × mature qualified-lead-to-collected-customer probability

Media CAC = media spend / new collected customers
CPQL = media spend / accepted qualified leads
Post-acquisition contribution = contribution before acquisition - all acquisition costs
```

Finance must specify commission treatment so costs are not subtracted twice. A zero denominator is undefined, not zero. Never use the property's AED purchase price as brokerage revenue or advertising conversion value.

**Illustrative arithmetic only:** a transaction produces AED 40,000 collected commission excluding VAT; agent payout is AED 24,000, referral payout AED 2,000 and direct costs AED 1,000. Contribution before acquisition is AED 13,000. With AED 1,000 non-media acquisition cost and AED 8,000 required retained contribution, allowable media CAC is AED 4,000. At a hypothetical mature 5% qualified-lead-to-collected-client rate, allowable CPQL is AED 200. These are not BlackSwan’s actual terms, performance or forecast.

Set separate economics for residential purchase, off-plan, residential rental, commercial purchase, commercial lease and owner-mandate acquisition. Model delayed developer commission collection, cancellation/refunds, instalments and overlapping acquisition costs. Expected pipeline value and collected value must not be merged into one unlabeled ROAS number.

### Budget algorithm

```text
1. Load Real Estate's explicitly approved envelope and cash-risk rules.
2. Exclude campaigns failing permissions, truthful offer, relevant inventory/service,
   page, measurement, delivery, consent, staffing or release gates.
3. Separate learning tests from mature commercial cohorts.
4. For mature cohorts, estimate marginal—not merely average—qualified demand and
   retained contribution, with uncertainty, lag, overlap and capacity constraints.
5. Propose bounded increments to positive-contribution opportunities.
6. Retain reserve where evidence, inventory or staffing is insufficient.
7. Submit changes for the authorized approval rule; log old/new state and read-back.
```

Do not import AED 3,000/day or the Business Setup 30%-leader rule into Real Estate without separate approval. Those are historical company-context rules, not this campaign’s budget authorization.

For most campaigns Google can spend up to twice the average daily budget on a day, with monthly limits commonly based on 30.4 times that average. Average budgets are not strict cash ceilings. Source: G9. Define planning envelope, monthly authorization and cash-risk tolerance separately; automated pause scripts can lag.

No universal conversion-count threshold or learning duration is assumed. Choose a bidding stage using actual volume, goal reliability, lag and economics. Broader matching, value bidding, Search Partners and new channels need their own experiment records and guardrails.

### 24.4 What must be measured

Preserve the existing canonical operational events. The following is the page-instrumentation contract, not a claim that all integrations already exist:

| Interaction or outcome | Event / record | Meaning and control |
|---|---|---|
| Page visit | `page_view` with landing-page context | One normal view per actual navigation; do not duplicate automatic and manual page views |
| Ordinary CTA click | `cta_click` | CTA, placement and destination are known; not automatically a lead |
| Call-button click | `phone_click` | Dial intent only; not proof of a connected call |
| WhatsApp button | `whatsapp_click` | Outbound action only; not proof a message was sent |
| Email button | `email_click` | Mail-client launch intent only; not proof of a delivered email |
| Form interaction | `form_start` | A visitor began the enquiry flow |
| Form failure | `form_error` | Sanitized error category only; no entered field contents |
| Accepted enquiry | Operational `form_accepted`; GA4 mapping `generate_lead` | Emit after durable server acceptance, not on submit click or thank-you reload |
| Brochure/floor-plan | `brochure_download`, `floorplan_view` | Record a click/open/confirmed response accurately; do not claim the document was read |
| Calculator/comparison | `calculator_complete`, `comparison_complete` | Valid result produced; not automatically qualified demand |
| Property details | `property_view` | Property ID and context where available |
| Viewing request | `viewing_request` | Request captured; not the same as a confirmed booking |
| Confirmed viewing | `viewing_booked`, later `viewing_attended` | Require booking-system or authorized outcome evidence |
| Main-site continuation | `lp_to_main_site` | Internal navigation; no new source, artificial visitor or fake homepage view |
| Connected conversation | `conversation_verified` | Supported call/message integration or documented operational evidence |
| Commercial stages | Existing `qualified_lead`, `mandate_signed`, `offer_submitted`, `transaction_closed`, `commission_collected` | Evidence-backed operational outcomes; separately authorized offline feedback only |

A specialized CTA event such as `phone_click` is the canonical click record for that interaction. Do not also count a generic `cta_click` as another CTA action in totals. When duplicate diagnostic representations are necessary, explicitly reconcile them rather than summing them.

Use native GA4 recommended-event mappings where appropriate, including `generate_lead` for the accepted-enquiry representation, without creating two bidding conversions for one enquiry. Source: G17. Select the primary bidding outcome by the approved campaign objective. Raw phone/WhatsApp clicks, document interactions and page views remain diagnostic by default.

Real call reporting depends on country/account and provider eligibility. Verify the applicable integration before claiming answered-call or duration tracking. Where it is unavailable, retain honest click measurement and an approved operational evidence route. Source: G16. Likewise, a WhatsApp conversation needs supported messaging evidence; a website click cannot establish it.

### 24.6 Privacy, consent and duplicate prevention

Consent controls must reflect the approved markets and actual implementation. Configure the required storage/data-use signals, including `analytics_storage`, `ad_storage`, `ad_user_data` and `ad_personalization`, and test initial, granted, denied and withdrawn states. Basic versus advanced consent mode is a reviewed choice, not an automatic preference. Consent Mode communicates choices; it does not itself obtain them. Source: G14.

Keep names, personal email addresses, personal phone numbers, free-text messages and other personal details out of GA4 event parameters, page URLs, titles and UTMs. Only allowlisted, non-sensitive metadata may go to general analytics. Enhanced-conversion user data, if separately approved and eligible, needs its own supported consent-aware path; hashing does not make it suitable for ordinary GA4 parameters. Source: G18.

Retain the existing durable backend, idempotency and outbox controls. Use a stable opaque conversion identifier in a supported deduplication field where applicable. Do not assume that a custom `event_id` universally deduplicates GA4, native Ads and imported events. Choose one Google Ads measurement route for each business action and test duplicates explicitly. Source: G12 and the baseline master.

No blanket promise of 100% visitor identification or attribution is permitted. Browser restrictions, consent choices, unavailable integrations and offline activity create gaps. Report observed, modeled and unknown outcomes separately. Do not require analytics consent in order to make an enquiry.

### 24.7 Mandatory implementation and release tests

Before a page is accepted, demonstrate native-page ownership, the actual editable content/route record, correct main domain, page-only noindex, useful content, functioning mobile layout and correct destinations.

```text

  -> native campaign page
  -> meaningful CTA
  -> public main-site page
  -> valid enquiry
  -> durable record and single canonical conversion
  -> email + Sheet delivery with the same Lead ID/source
```

Repeat for direct traffic without click identifiers; consent granted/denied/withdrawn; invalid form fields; double-click; reload; network retry; email/Sheet outage; slow mobile; external booking return where used; unchanged main-site public forms; and staging/test traffic exclusion. Validate Google tag firing with Tag Assistant/GTM Preview, GA4 DebugView, Ads diagnostics and backend/delivery logs. Do not test by repeatedly clicking live advertisements or training production bidding with synthetic leads.

Deliver a page-level tracking matrix: URL, CMS/native page ID, campaign/page IDs, GTM version, GA4 stream, conversion-action mapping, CTA inventory, source persistence result, form acceptance result, email/Sheet receipt, consent result, duplicate result, mobile result, reviewer and evidence reference. A page is not complete merely because it looks correct or contains a tracking script.
