# Independent veterinary complete-title semantic review

All **51 complete source titles were individually read** in three bounded sets of 17, including every long, symbol-bearing and compatibility-held title. The result is **51 `PROPOSE_FULL_EXACT_RESEARCH`, zero semantic `HOLD`**. These are source-supply semantic proposals only. **Admissions, approved exclusions and deployments remain zero.**

[semantic-decisions.json](semantic-decisions.json) contains each exact source ordinal, qualification number, untouched original literal, original normalized title, source-line range, literal hash, individual semantic reason and separately preserved compatibility diagnostics. It is the per-title decision record. The qualification title itself is the candidate boundary; normalization is recorded for identity, not proposed as a rewritten candidate.

## Literal meaning and protected intent

Each complete title explicitly states a Certificate, Diploma, Award or qualification End Point Assessment. The subject matter identifies veterinary occupational learning or assessment. This conclusion was reached by reading each full literal, not by approving every occurrence of the word veterinary or selecting the 19 compatibility-clear titles.

The reviews retain the occupational distinctions in reception, nursing assistance, nursing, equine and small-animal routes, medicines competence, clinical coaching, specialist care learning, physiotherapy, hydrotherapy, educator training, client relations, wellbeing, bereavement support, triage and first aid. Care-related phrases are interpreted only inside their complete credential title. Actual clinical care, emergency treatment, medicines supply, rehabilitation, mental-health or bereavement support, and veterinary businesses seeking premises remain protected. Work Based and Practice within these full qualifications do not establish property intent.

`SA`, `EQ`, `BVRA`, `VTEC`, `ST0343`, provider prefixes, `Level2`, parenthetical text, en dashes, double spaces and trailing spaces remain untouched. No abbreviation is expanded. The review does not infer equivalence or proven distinct demand between provider-prefixed titles, old/new routes, VTEC/non-VTEC titles, Award/Certificate routes, SA/Small Animal, EQ/Equine or BVRA/non-BVRA forms. These require later membership, variant, protected-query and scope checks.
