## Review boundaries

- Explicit job, vacancy, internship and application wording supports a narrow draft employment proposal. No generic `job`, `salary`, `career` or `vacancy` exclusion is created.
- Professional classes, exam preparation, courses, training and explicit entry-to-profession wording are draft education proposals. An observed keyword mentioning certification, a named regulator, a foreign location or an hour requirement does not prove a real course, law, approval or licensing rule.
- Credentials and qualifications can mean obtaining a credential or checking a professional. Fragments, programs, seminars, study and unexplained acronyms can leave purpose unresolved. They stay held.
- Named-course interpretations rely on the linked provider evidence, not discovery-batch membership. The14revised proposals remain executing-agent inferences requiring independent review. No affiliation, course quality, legal approval or Black Swan service is inferred.
