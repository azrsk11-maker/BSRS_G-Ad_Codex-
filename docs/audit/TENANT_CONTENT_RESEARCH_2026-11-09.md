## Draft copy for factual review

### Prepare for a Dubai rental viewing

Before booking a viewing, write down your preferred areas, annual rent budget, property type and latest move-in date. Rank the features you need, such as parking, lift access or space for working at home. Ask for the specific unit, viewing time and current availability to be confirmed. Keep written notes so you can compare units using the same criteria.

At the viewing, check the unit's condition, noise, cooling and access. Ask who handles repairs and how to report them. If you want to proceed, request the proposed rent, payment dates, deposit and other charges in writing before deciding.

### Plan the handover after you choose a home

Confirm the date and time for collecting keys, any building move-in arrangements, and who will be present. Agree how the condition of the unit will be recorded and how to report anything that needs attention. Check which utility accounts, access cards and parking arrangements you need.

Dubai Land Department provides the official service for registering or renewing a tenancy contract. Check the relevant channel and required documents with DLD. Use the appropriate official workflow for identity documents, contract uploads and payments. [DLD tenancy contract service](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/register-renew-ejari-contract/)
