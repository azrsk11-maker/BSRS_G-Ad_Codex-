## Registering a completed-property sale

**Draft title:** Registering a completed-property sale in Dubai

Dubai Land Department provides a sale registration service for buyers, sellers and their legally authorized representatives. Its guidance describes submitting documents for verification at a Real Estate Registration Trustee centre. The department lists identity documentation for individuals and a developer e-NOC in freehold areas. Check the official requirements for the transaction before preparing documents or paying fees. DLD lists a separate related service for mortgaged property. [DLD Property Sale Registration](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/property-sale-registration)

**Review before use:** Confirm which part of the process Black Swan can support and who owns the handoff to the relevant provider. Use the sale registration route for ordinary sale content. The separately named title-transfer service describes transfers based on official letters and should receive its own scope review. [DLD Title Transfer Application](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/request-for-transfer-of-ownership/)

## Checking a Dubai title deed

**Draft title:** Where to verify a Dubai title deed

Dubai Land Department provides a service to verify the validity of a title deed it issued. The service is available through the DLD website and Dubai REST. Use the department's own service to enter the requested details. A deed validity result answers that specific check. For other questions about the transaction, consult the appropriate official service. [DLD Verify Title Deed](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/title-deed-verification-overview/)

**Review before use:** Keep the official link distinct from any brokerage enquiry. The enquiry form should not request title deed numbers, identity numbers, scans or credentials for this page.

## Checking a Dubai property's recorded status

**Draft title:** Where to check a Dubai property's recorded status

Dubai Land Department offers a separate property status enquiry. Its interface accepts several property identifiers, including a title deed or a property number. Use the official service and choose the lookup method for the details available to you. [DLD Property Status Enquiry](https://dubailand.gov.ae/en/eservices/property-status-overview/property-status/)

**Review before use:** Hold any claims about a specific property's mortgage, restrictions, ownership or availability until checked in the authorized workflow. Keep property identifiers and lookup results outside the public page, optional measurement and the standard enquiry submission.
