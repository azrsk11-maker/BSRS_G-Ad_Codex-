## Implemented behavior

- [Content evidence engine](../../tools/content-evidence/README.md): exact stable page/campaign identities, content hashes, complete evidence-record digests, sources, scope, nominated update owner, dated independent review, expiry and sanitized per-page suppression reasons. Changing a record or reapproving it cannot reuse the old page review. Public research alone is insufficient for publication. Genuine service/advisory evidence does not require buyer stock; disclosed unavailable inventory needs reviewed disclosure and current alternatives or a real advisory service.
- [Website release integration](../../tools/release-control/README.md): `SOURCE_TRUTH` now requires a committed per-page content inventory, exact individual HTML/Markdown artifact hashes and a private dated evidence ledger. It recomputes validity using the current evaluation clock, rejects precomputed PASS summaries and missing/changed artifacts, and prevents a gate receipt or approval from outliving the underlying source. Ledger source/review observations cannot occur after the receipt. UTC aliases are compared without changing signed/hash-bound ledger bytes.
- [Creative preparation engine](../../tools/creative-validation/README.md): independently supplied exact group/native-page/company/language bindings, reviewed commit and reviewer allowlist; control/challenger preparation, count/width/duplicate checks, documented experiments and scoped copy/claim/combination/asset receipts. Dependent reviews cannot precede the evidence they claim to review. Ambiguous glyphs and unsupported dynamic templates hold instead of claiming platform compliance.
- Both new focused suites are mandatory in the existing Foundation runner. Existing native fixtures, signed wire/schema contracts, the frozen launch identities and serving-child counts remain unchanged.

## Remaining boundaries and dependencies

The engines validate supplied evidence consistency. They cannot authenticate a consultant or reviewer identity, discover every rendered asset or factual claim, prove source completeness, establish jurisdiction-specific permission or marketing rights, or grant an owner approval. A suppression manifest is read-only; no live page is automatically unpublished by this code. Runtime enforcement, actual producer/custody evidence and separately authorized deployment remain required.

Master section 12 explicitly separates challenger preparation from simultaneous serving. Preparing two candidates does not require changing the six currently frozen serving ads or their readback counts. This is a documented interpretation, not an owner decision or a live experiment authorization.
