## Classification and review

Three separately owned batches covered the final 5,365 remaining phrases (1,789 / 1,788 / 1,788). Every literal phrase was read in bounded chunks, assigned an explicit draft decision and checked against its source order. Read-only peer review covered every batch; parent review checked exact coverage, scope, suspicious semantic boundaries and consistency with earlier records. This agent review does not satisfy independent human PR approval or business admission.

The final batch contributes 2,009 positive-review, 3,309 held and 47 exact-negative proposals. Parent consistency review also moved three prior positives to HOLD. Parent review applied 18 targeted record edits: 13 positive-to-HOLD corrections, three refinements to existing held entity reasons and two mall-protection additions. The final eight positive-to-HOLD changes in batch 3 were separately reviewed for entity identity, unit form and yearly terms before canonical acceptance.

Holds preserve possible demand; they are not rejections or exclusions. Key boundaries:

- Discovery batch provenance does not establish intent. Generic rent can concern vehicles; generic property rent can span tenant/landlord and residential/commercial services.
- Standalone bedroom, studio-type and studio-villa wording can leave the actual unit/room/annex service unresolved.
- Shop sale may mean premises title or an operating business. Direct property-buying and fast-sale claims need an actual provider/offer; brokerage alone establishes neither.
- Named entity identity, portals, price/affordability, monthly/yearly/short stays, special terms, land-service taxonomy and unclear roles stay held when unresolved. Literal names are not corrected into invented entities.
- Residential wording in an airport-free-zone location requires factual location/use evidence; a hold asserts no zoning prohibition or legal conclusion.
- Lower-budget property demand stays protected. Cheap or affordable is not a blanket negative.
- `shopping mall for sale in dubai` and `mall for sale in dubai` retain possible commercial-property intent. Retail shopping queries cannot justify blanket shopping/mall exclusions.
